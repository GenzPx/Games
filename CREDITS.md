## Physiology references (not affiliated)
Field reports and published values used to tune SpO₂, heart rate, death-zone
behavior, and the “treadmill + straw” exertion model:

- David Breashears — “running on a treadmill and breathing through a straw”
- Shaunna Burke, Everest 2005 — body breaking down on a clock
- AMREE / Operation Everest II — VO₂max and summit saturation
- Recreational pulse-ox series: ~97% sea level → ~81% at 4,200 m → ~55–60% above 8,000 m
- HAPE / HACE clinical descriptions; death-zone survival window ~16–20 h
- Indonesian field notes on hypothermia (Merbabu, Cartenz) — shivering, slurred speech, sudden collapse
